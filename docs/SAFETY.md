# XAAA Safety

XAAA produces simulated or mediated experience. It must not be confused with operational authority.

## Constraints

- No real emergency command.
- No medical, legal or financial decision automation.
- Counterfactuals are pedagogical, not proof of causality.
- The system must clearly distinguish simulation from reality.
- High-risk domains require expert validation.
- User consent is required before using collective traces.
